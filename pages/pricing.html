Common Questions

Everything you need to know

How does the 90-day trial work?

Launch the tool and your trial starts automatically — stored in your browser. No account or credit card needed. The trial badge shows days remaining. When it expires, purchase a licence key to continue.

How do I get my licence key?

Complete checkout via Stripe. Your licence key is generated automatically and emailed to you from support@agilityops.com.au within seconds. Paste it into the tool's licence modal to activate.

What's the difference between Starter and Team?

Starter is for an individual practitioner. Team covers your whole delivery team with shared access. Both include all features — no capability gating between tiers.

Is my data safe?

All InSite tools run entirely in your browser. No data is sent to our servers. Your data stays on your device in localStorage — export to JSON at any time for a portable backup.

What happens when my licence expires?

The tool shows an EXPIRED badge and prompts for a new key. Your data is preserved. Contact us for a renewal key or purchase via this page — it arrives by email in seconds.

Can I get an invoice for accounting?

Yes. Stripe generates a tax invoice automatically and emails it to you. If you need a formal PO-based invoice, contact support@agilityops.com.au.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes — within 14 days of purchase if the tool doesn't work as described. Contact support@agilityops.com.au with your order details and we'll process a full refund.

I need a multi-year or enterprise agreement.

Contact us at support@agilityops.com.au. We offer 2-year licences, enterprise agreements, and volume pricing for large teams and consulting partners.

Questions before you buy?

Our team is happy to walk you through the tool, answer questions, or help you choose the right licence.
