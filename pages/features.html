Powerful features for portfolio success

Gain complete visibility across your Jira portfolio with intelligent dashboards, dependency mapping, and real-time program health monitoring.

📊
Cross-Project Dashboards
Portfolio Visibility

One view. Every project.

Stop jumping between Jira boards to understand your portfolio. PortfolioInSite aggregates project data into a single, powerful dashboard that shows status, velocity, and risk across all teams. See progress at a glance and drill down into details when you need them.

  • Unified project status overview at portfolio level
  • Customizable dashboard layouts for different stakeholders
  • Real-time data synced directly from Jira
  • Filter by team, label, component, or project status
  • Track key metrics: velocity, burn-down, cycle time
  • Responsive design works across all devices
🔗
Dependency Mapping
Dependency Intelligence

See the connections others miss

Cross-team dependencies are the silent killers of delivery timelines. PortfolioInSite surfaces every connection and highlights risks before they impact your schedule. Visualize complex dependency chains and identify critical path items instantly.

  • Visual dependency graph with interactive exploration
  • Automated risk detection for blocking issues
  • Cross-project and cross-team dependency alerts
  • Impact analysis for proposed changes
  • Critical path identification across portfolio
  • Dependency health scores and trend analysis
📈
Program Health Monitoring
Program Health

Confidence through clarity

Give stakeholders the visibility they need without the noise. Automated health scores, trend analysis, and executive-ready summaries make reporting effortless. Track program health with RAG status, risk indicators, and predictive insights.

  • Real-time RAG status indicators (Red, Amber, Green)
  • Trend and trajectory analysis with predictive warnings
  • Auto-generated status reports for stakeholders
  • Shareable health dashboards with customizable views
  • Risk scoring and escalation workflows
  • Historical health tracking and audit trail
👥
Resource Planning
Team Capacity Management

Optimize team utilization

Understand team capacity and allocation across all projects in real time. Make informed decisions about resource distribution and workload balance. Identify over-allocated teams and bottlenecks before they impact delivery.

  • Team capacity planning and allocation tracking
  • Workload balancing across multiple projects
  • Resource availability and utilization metrics
  • Skill-based resource mapping and recommendations
  • Capacity forecasting based on historical data
  • Bottleneck detection and conflict resolution
🎯
Strategic Alignment
Strategic Connection

Align work with organizational goals

Map every project and initiative to strategic objectives and OKRs. Ensure every team's effort connects to organizational goals with complete traceability. Show leadership exactly how portfolio work drives business outcomes.

  • OKR mapping to projects and epics
  • Strategic objective tracking and alignment verification
  • Goal progress indicators linked to portfolio metrics
  • Traceability from stories to strategic objectives
  • Alignment health scoring by program and team
  • Executive summaries showing strategic impact
📋
Executive Reporting
Leadership Reporting

Beautiful reports, ready to share

Generate professionally formatted portfolio reports for leadership in minutes, not days. Auto-generated summaries, progress snapshots, and risk highlights come ready to present. Customize report templates to match your governance needs.

  • Auto-generated executive summaries
  • Professional report templates (PDF, PowerPoint, HTML)
  • Progress snapshots with key metrics and trends
  • Risk highlights and escalation summaries
  • Customizable report scheduling and distribution
  • Stakeholder-specific view filters and branding
