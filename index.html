How It Works

Up and running in minutes

PortfolioInSite integrates seamlessly with your existing Jira setup.

1

Install from Marketplace

Add PortfolioInSite to your Jira Cloud instance with one click from the Atlassian Marketplace.

2

Connect Your Projects

PortfolioInSite automatically discovers your projects and begins aggregating data across your portfolio.

3

Get Visibility

View portfolio dashboards, dependency maps, and program health metrics — all from within Jira.
